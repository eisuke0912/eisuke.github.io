# Eisuke Kobayashi

I am a Master of Public Administration candidate at Columbia University's School of International and Public Affairs (SIPA), concentrating in Data Science for Policy with a minor in International Finance and Economic Policy. Before SIPA, I spent seven years at Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI), working on human resources, industrial, and energy policy.

My interests include population aging, industrial and small business policy, regional economic development, innovation, and human capital policy.

## Projects

- **[The Energy Market Dashboard](personal_writeup.md)**: An interactive Streamlit dashboard, built with a teammate, on how electricity prices in New York respond to demand and fuel costs, backed by an automated BigQuery pipeline.
- **[Human Capital and Economic Growth](project%203.ipynb)**: A cross-country regression of public education spending on total factor productivity growth, using World Bank and Our World in Data series.
- **[SAT Scores and Economic Status](project%202.ipynb)**: An analysis of the relationship between average SAT scores and the share of low-income students across New York City schools, using NYC Open Data.
